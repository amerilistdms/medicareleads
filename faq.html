Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I find Medicare prospects?

Medicare prospects can be identified using targeted consumer data based on factors such as age, geography and other available demographic criteria. FindMedicareProspects.com by AmeriList allows you to describe the audience you’re trying to reach and request a complimentary market analysis for your territory.

Can I find people who are Turning 65?

Yes. Turning 65 prospecting allows insurance marketers to target consumers approaching age 65 within specific geographic areas. Depending on the available data, audiences can be developed around birth date, age and location.

Can I search for Medicare prospects by ZIP Code?

Yes. Medicare prospect audiences can be researched by ZIP Code, county, city, state, radius or combinations of geographic areas.

Can I get phone numbers for Medicare prospects?

Phone information may be available depending on the audience and campaign. Tell us how you plan to use the data and we’ll research the appropriate options.

Can I get email addresses?

Email information may be available for certain audiences and permitted marketing applications. Email can also be combined with other available contact channels.

Can you provide Medicare mailing lists?

Yes. Targeted Medicare and senior prospect audiences can be developed for direct mail campaigns. Additional printing and mailing services may also be available.

How many Medicare prospects are in my area?

That depends on your geography and targeting criteria. Use our Medicare Prospect Finder to enter your target market and audience. We’ll prepare a complimentary market analysis showing the available counts.

Do I have to buy a list to get a count?

No. You can request a complimentary market analysis before deciding whether you want to move forward with a prospect list or marketing campaign.

Are these exclusive Medicare leads?

Not necessarily. FindMedicareProspects.com by AmeriList primarily helps marketers identify targeted prospect audiences. Prospect data should not automatically be interpreted as consumers who specifically requested an insurance quote or expressed an immediate interest in purchasing a Medicare plan.

Can you help me determine who I should target?

Yes. If you’re unsure which audience makes the most sense, tell us about your insurance products, geography and marketing goals. Our team can help research available targeting options.
